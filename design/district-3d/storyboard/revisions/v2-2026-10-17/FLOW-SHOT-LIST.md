## Sixteen causal actions to draw

1. Track motion into eye contact (S01 A–B).
2. Anticipate and raise the packet (S01 C).
3. Present and hand it to the courier (S01 D).
4. Catch it in the Research tray (S02 A).
5. Sweep/open/inspect its ribbed core (S02 B).
6. Reseal and lift it (S02 C).
7. Carry it along the Research–Forge road (S02 D approach).
8. Catch it with the Forge latch (S02 D arrival).
9. Brace a frame around its core (S03 A).
10. Hinge parts into a fixture, collar raised (S03 B).
11. Carry to Verification and expose the scan gap (S03 C).
12. Seat that collar, closing the scan band (S03 D).
13. Dock the same fixture at Preview (S04 A).
14. Open gateway latches/struts and energize inlays (S04 B).
15. Reveal the built spatial project through the aperture (S04 C).
16. Actuate its attached control and show its physical response (S04 D).

Transit may be time-compressed, with a departure/arrival match cut, but packet identity, receiving custody and travel direction remain explicit. A machine answers the preceding contact; no unrelated light flash stands in for every action.
