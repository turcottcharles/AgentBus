## Continuity and production locks

- Hold one packet’s custody through Research and Workshop; keep the same core in the assembled fixture. The visible defect motivates repair and a second scan.
- Short inserts supply anticipation; the wide reveal must have room to land. Avoid treating every frame as a new medium-wide three-quarter screenshot.
- Eye and hand close-ups create character intimacy. Low travel establishes the working route. Mechanism details show cause and consequence. The wide payoff restores spatial scale.
- Use the same saved host and district geometry. Dolly = move camera toward/away; truck/tracking = travel alongside; crane = lift camera. The later overhead management view is explicitly player-chosen.
- Subject motion and camera direction are separate. Keep the lower conversation area usable from frame one; drafts never pause or dispatch.
- All staged work is DEMO / STORYBOARD. A ring, light or character pose never creates a real test result. Real project state needs its actual project/run/candidate evidence.
- Floating comic scoreboard/card treatment stays rejected. Preserve the historical archives, but use the current spatial result and current shot list.
- This pass directs artists; it does not establish measured engagement or completed product gameplay. All 3D frame capture uses hardware GPU.
