# Mechanical presence, then a person

Owner direction forwarded from the other chat and confirmed here: establish the
robot through a low, close mechanical detail, briefly suggesting power or danger.
Delay the face reveal; then curiosity, vulnerability and helpfulness reframe him.
This is original staging, not a reconstruction of a Short Circuit shot.

For this hovering host, preserve his model rather than adding borrowed treads.
The owner found the initial drive-vane opening tasteful but insufficiently
intimidating. The approved next direction adds three compact industrial rocket
thrusters below the original body: heat-darkened exhaust bells, copper feed
lines, protective ribs and hot exhaust. Ignition, a brief surge and precise hover
provide the character-establishing boot shot. Follow with a
delayed profile reveal: the first checking glance and asymmetric brow turn
machinery into someone we want to follow. The mechanical inserts establish the
character like a heavy work boot before we see who is wearing it. Keep the
antenna compact and cute.

## Mechanical opening motion study

The [12-second motion study](storyboard/mechanical-opening.mp4) uses the original
host and actual district in full color. Play **Mechanical opening** on the
[shared shot board](storyboard/index.html). Its original synthesized sound pass
adds a low drive rumble, copper servo, restrained stop and warm reveal tone.
The thruster revision adds an ignition bark and exhaust hiss. Cold, low-key steel
light and a brief amber ignition wash establish danger; a softer warm face light
rises during the profile reveal. This is an acting/mood transition, never a
signal claiming a successful job. Hardware and all light/exhaust pictures render
on the GPU. The original host GLB remains unchanged; the thruster pack is a saved
additive source in `storyboard/host-thrusters.js`.
No film audio or footage is used. The scene and renderer are saved alongside
the movie as `mechanical-opening-scene.js` and `render-mechanical-opening.cjs`.
The [machine-readable shot list](MECHANICAL-OPENING.json) records the captured
camera endpoints. [GPU frame provenance](storyboard/mechanical-opening-provenance.json)
records all 288 frames and the source hashes. Regenerate with
`node design/district-3d/storyboard/render-mechanical-opening.cjs`.
