## Color, light and physical form

Ink and blue slate carry most surfaces. Cyan and violet provide cool energy; amber windows and copper hardware provide warmth. Pearl ceramic gives the host a readable foreground silhouette. UI accents remain `#64e4ed` cyan, `#c99bff` violet and `#ffbe63` amber over `#0b1021` ink / `#11192d` panels. Green `#83ddb3` and rose `#ff8b9c` accompany explicit outcome/error words. Shader colors are embedded PBR values; UI hex colors are not a substitute for the authored material values.

Use beveled layered shells, stepped roofs, buttresses, visible joints, rounded optical hardware and substantial terraces. Every road, arch and facility has depth and a physical support. Favor a few readable shapes at management distance, with smaller seams and fittings rewarding a close camera. Maintain clean silhouette gaps around the portal, dome and host hands.

Materials combine dark structural alloy, rough blue-slate foundations, petrol/teal/violet enamel, warm copper roofs, cream ceramic trim, deep teal optical glass and small emissive inlays. Keep roughness and metalness differences visible through GPU environment reflections. Cool ambient light and warm practical lights should model the geometry. Restrained bloom supports luminous materials while preserving their edges, dark recesses and readable controls.

## Motion and truth

Cinematic moves ease over about 2.6 seconds. The host has a soft 3–4 second hover, slower head attention, small independent hand motions and a gently turning core. One welcoming gesture makes it feel present. The management camera stays settled so selection remains predictable. Reduced motion preserves static poses and immediate text/state updates.

Ambient city life and host personality are scenery. The prebuilt app transformation stays visibly labeled **DEMO**. Actual run cues require the current project/run identity and an observed or clearly agent-reported state. An accepted command means waiting to start; an observed file change means a file changed; source checks and browser checks keep separate labels. A real pass may receive one brief acknowledgment after a live transition. Historical results and repeated snapshot polls do not replay it. Failed, stopping, interrupted and unknown states settle the work effects and preserve the diagnostic words.

The renderer presents state and never dispatches commands. Facility selection is a view choice; explicit controls own operations. The portal remains scenery until an actual project/run/candidate-bound preview is opened. Preserve the inspected bytes, candidate identity and evidence behind every visible outcome. See [activity mapping](ACTIVITY-MAPPING.md) and [story boundaries](WYSIWYG-STORY.md) for the operational rules.
