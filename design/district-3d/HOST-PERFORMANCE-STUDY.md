## The acting idea

**An event arrives. The face registers it. Uncertainty motivates investigation. The dish resolves a direction. The character commits.**

The sequence should read with sound off. Optics and brow move first; the head follows; the body and hand finish the action. Holds let the viewer read the change. Continuous idle motion must not drown out a small reaction.

| Beat | Proposed time | Facial articulation | Sensor / body consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attend | 0–0.75 s | Relaxed brows; stable optical aim and a small hover. | Antenna stays folded. |
| Notice | 0.75–1.35 s | Both pupils/iris rings shift toward the cue; one brow lifts before the head. | The body remains settled. |
| Uncertain | 1.35–2.2 s | One deliberate checking glance and a small asymmetric brow change; a head tilt follows. | This motivates the sensor, rather than random gadget movement. |
| Deploy | 2.2–3.4 s | Keep the eyes on the unresolved cue. | Mast extends; six dish petals unfold. Camera eases back to keep the attachment in view. |
| Search | 3.4–5.8 s | Gaze follows the dish with a small delay; the brow remains questioning. | Dish performs a bounded left/right search with clear reversals. |
| Lock | 5.8–6.4 s | Optics settle on one direction; brows approach a resolved pose. | Dish stops searching and holds its aim. A brief restrained feed light acknowledges attention lock. |
| Commit | 6.4–8.2 s | Eyes lead; head turns; asymmetry relaxes and a small nod resolves. | Body turns after the face, without a sudden synchronized snap. |
| Follow | 8.2–10 s | Maintain the target eye-line. | Host floats toward the selected direction while the dish stays aimed. |

These durations are an audition, not an animation law. Adjust to readability at the actual screen size. A two-frame optical lead at 24 fps can establish attention; a several-frame brow/head lag makes the response readable. Do not put every joint on one sine wave.

## Directions to the animator

- **Noticing:** a quick optical shift gives the event a target; a brow registers the change; the head catches up. The cue should have an identifiable cause.
- **Uncertainty:** use one small asymmetry and one checking glance, then hold. Repeated random darting reads as noise.
- **Investigation:** the antenna deploys because the first look did not resolve the cue. Let extension, unfolding, search and lock be distinct actions.
- **Resolution:** remove the searching motion. Stillness is the payoff before the turn and travel.
- **Follow-through:** eyes, head and body have different onset times. Hands participate only when the action calls for them.
- **Listening:** keep attention on the person. Reduce background gestures while a person is speaking or composing.

## Literal-work truth

The performance study is staged DEMO. An antenna lock means the character selected an attention direction; it is not a completed research job, a granted permission or a passed test. In real work, movement and result reactions must use the same scoped project/run/candidate state as the GUI and CLI. Uncertainty must not be animated as success. Typing remains a draft. An explicitly committed request owns dispatch.
